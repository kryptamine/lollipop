# Advice accepts the Riot policy risk

Lollipop gives Advice: instructions a model writes during and around a game. This replaces ADR 0008's rule that the overlay shows measurements with no instructions, and its note that Riot's audit is binding for that grey area. The owner has read the policy and takes responsibility for it; the data sources in ADR 0008 still hold.

## Consequences

- Riot may reject the product in an audit or act against the key or the account. This risk is accepted.
- Design work on Advice does not wait on, or ask about, Riot policy.
- Markers stay measurements: only Advice gives instructions.
